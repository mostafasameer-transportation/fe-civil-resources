Exam Strategy

Study Tips for the FE Civil

Practical advice for a 110-question, 5 hour 20 minute computer-based exam.

01
Know the Handbook Cold

The FE Reference Handbook is your only allowed resource. Practice navigating it quickly under timed conditions so you can locate formulas in seconds.

02
Weight Your Study Time

Focus on Mathematics, Statics, Structural Analysis, and Geotechnical Engineering — they carry the most questions. Don't neglect Transportation and Environmental.

03
Practice Timed Sets

With ~2.9 minutes per question, simulate exam conditions. Use the 800 solved problems book to build speed and accuracy simultaneously.

04
Flag & Return

Never get stuck. Flag difficult questions and return to them. First-pass efficiency often determines whether you finish the exam in time.

05
Units & Conversions

Most errors on the FE Civil come from unit mistakes. Build the habit of tracking units at every step, especially in fluid mechanics and statics problems.

06
Use NCEES Practice Exam

The official NCEES practice exam is the closest simulation. Take it under full exam conditions at least two weeks before your test date to calibrate.
